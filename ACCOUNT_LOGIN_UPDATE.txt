CYBER//NET ACCOUNT LOGIN UPDATE

The login screen now includes:
- LOGIN: sign in with an existing account.
- CREATE ACCOUNT: register a new account.
- Player name is optional; if left blank, the account username is used.
- ENTER NETWORK is available after successful authentication.

Accounts are stored through the configured DATABASE_URL (Neon) when set.
Keep DATABASE_URL configured on Render and in your local .env file.
Do not share your database connection string or password.
